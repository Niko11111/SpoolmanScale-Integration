# Partner servers

SpoolmanScale works with any server that implements this specification. You
do not need to ask anyone: implement [Part 1](inventory/spec.md), run the
checker, and every scale can use your server today.

If you want more than compatibility, we can work together. A partner server
is not just reachable from the scale, it is part of it.

## What a partner server gets

- **Its own entry on the scale.** Users pick your server by name when they set
  up the scale, next to Spoolman, FilaMan and BamBuddy, and the scale names
  your server wherever it speaks of it.
- **Your look.** A colour palette in your brand colours, on the display and in
  the web interface.
- **A page in the manual** that walks your users through the setup with your
  screenshots, and a place in the manual's overview of backends and in the
  README.
- **Tested with every release.** Each firmware release is checked against your
  server before it goes out, so your users are not the ones who find a break.
- **A direct line, with priority.** Questions about the scale, and issues your
  users report, reach the person who builds the firmware and are handled
  first. You hear about changes that touch your integration before they ship.

## What we ask of you

- A test account or instance the release tests can run against.
- A contact for questions, and for the day something breaks.
- Your logo and brand colours, with permission to show them in the firmware
  and the manual.
- A short note before you change something the integration depends on.
