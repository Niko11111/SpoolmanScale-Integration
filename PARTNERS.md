# Partner servers

SpoolmanScale works with any server that implements this specification. You
do not need to ask anyone: implement [Part 1](inventory/spec.md), run the
checker, and every scale can use your server today. Where it runs is up to
you: on the user's own network, as Spoolman, FilaMan and BamBuddy do, or as an
online service over HTTPS.

If you want more than compatibility, there are two further levels.

**Integrated.** For self-hosted and small non-commercial projects. Your server
gets its own entry on the scale, with its name and your colours, and a page in
the manual. You send the palette and the texts as a pull request and we take
them over. No agreement is needed.

**Partner.** For servers with a large user base and for commercial services.
Everything from Integrated, plus a test before every firmware release, a
direct line with priority, and a place in the README and the manual's
overview. Partnerships are agreed one by one, based on how widely a server is
used, how actively it is maintained and what it brings to people who own a
scale. A large non-commercial project can be a partner as well. We agree the
details together.

## What an integrated server gets

- **Its own entry on the scale.** Users pick your server by name when they set
  up the scale, next to Spoolman, FilaMan and BamBuddy, and the scale names
  your server wherever it speaks of it.
- **Your look.** A colour palette in your brand colours, on the display and in
  the web interface.
- **A page in the manual** that walks your users through the setup with your
  screenshots.

What we ask: a contact for questions, your logo and brand colours with
permission to show them in the firmware and the manual, and a short note
before you change something the integration depends on.

## What a partner server gets in addition

- **Tested with every release.** Each firmware release is checked against your
  server before it goes out, so your users are not the ones who find a break.
- **A direct line, with priority.** Questions about the scale, and issues your
  users report, reach the person who builds the firmware and are handled
  first. You hear about changes that touch your integration before they ship.
- **A place in the README** and in the manual's overview of backends.

What we ask in addition: a test account or instance the release tests can run
against, and a contact for the day something breaks.
